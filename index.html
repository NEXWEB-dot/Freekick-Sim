How To Strike & Curve With Your Foot

Step 01

Position & Aim Foot

Move your mouse horizontally across the screen to angle your kicking boot. Angle inward for inside curler, square for power laces, or outward for a trivela bend.

Step 02

Wind Up & Pull Back

Click and pull your mouse backwards (or hold the Kick button) to cock the player's kicking leg high behind the ball. The further you wind up, the greater the shot power.

Step 03

Swing Through & Strike

Swipe your mouse forward swiftly towards the ball! The leg whips through, striking the ball at the exact angle and velocity of your swing, curving it dynamically in mid-air.
